Sexual and mental health services for sex workers in Thailand

Health & Help is an open dataset of organisations in Thailand that provide sexual or mental health services and whose own pages, or other published sources, name sex workers among the people that the organisations serve. Our project used a large language model once to search the web and to record each organisation with a sentence copied from a web page as evidence. A script with no AI then checked whether each copied sentence appears on the web page that the model cited. A small model now in development will answer questions offline on the phones that people already use.

A script checked every copied sentence

Each record in the dataset includes a sentence that the large model copied from a web page as evidence that the organisation serves sex workers. A script with no AI downloaded each of those web pages and searched the page for the copied sentence.

    What the dataset never records

    The dataset records only contact details that organisations publish themselves.

    • No address of a shelter or safe house
    • No names of staff, volunteers or clients
    • Location at province and district level only
    • Map pins at provincial capitals

    No map coordinates · Published contacts only

    In development

    A small model will answer on the phone

    A small classifier that runs in the browser will match a question to the checked records and answer only from those records, with no internet connection.

    • Thai
    • English
    • Burmese
    • SMS simulator for basic phones
